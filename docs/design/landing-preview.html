About

One accurate record, held securely by the department

This is the official register of postgraduate students in the Department of Education. It keeps one accurate, current record for each student.

It replaces the shared spreadsheet the department has been using. That spreadsheet worked, but it had real problems: anyone with the link could open it, several versions circulated at once, and it was never clear which one was current. A single secure system fixes all three.

Who can see your record

One person, and no one else.

The Postgraduate Coordinator, who maintains the register.

Students cannot see the register. You cannot see another student’s record, and no other student can see yours. Every time anyone opens the register, the system records who it was and when.

What is coming later

Future versions will add proposal uploads, supervisor access, and progress tracking through to defence. Those are not part of this version.

What we never collect

No national ID number, no date of birth, no photograph, no financial information, no biometric data. We do not use your information for advertising, we do not share it outside the department, and we never sell it.

Why it matters

Why we’re asking, and why it helps you

Six things an accurate record actually changes for you.

Your progress is tracked accurately

When your stage, title, and supervisors are current, your supervisors and the department can see where you are and support you properly.

You only tell us once

Submit your details a single time. The department uses the same record for all internal coordination, so you are not asked for the same information again and again.

You hear about things that matter

Your email address lets us tell you about seminars, deadlines, funding calls, and defence schedules. Nothing else.

Your rights are protected

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 you can ask for a copy of your record, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We respond within the time limits the law sets.

Fewer delays at milestones

Accurate records mean less back-and-forth when you reach defence and graduation.

A connection after you graduate

If you choose to opt in, we will invite you to alumni events, mentorship, and research collaborations after you finish.

Protection

How we protect your information

Security

Your information travels to us over an encrypted connection using TLS 1.3. Security credentials for staff accounts are encrypted in our database. The register itself is held on access-controlled systems.

Access control

Only the Postgraduate Coordinator can see your record. Every time the register is opened, exported, or changed, the system records it.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your record, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact our Data Protection Officer at info@learning254.com, or use the Data Request form. The law gives us seven days to respond to a request for a copy and fourteen days to correct or delete.

How long we keep it

We keep your record until you graduate, and then for five more years. After that it is deleted automatically.
